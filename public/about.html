About Ontario Tech Media Pass

We’re the creative storytellers behind Ontario Tech’s most memorable moments — capturing, producing, and sharing student life through media.

Who We Are

Ontario Tech Media Pass is a student-led organization dedicated to highlighting the vibrancy and achievements of our campus community. Our team of photographers, videographers, editors, and designers work together to tell authentic stories from every corner of Ontario Tech University.

Our Mission

To empower Ontario Tech students to share their experiences and creativity through impactful digital storytelling — connecting campus life through the lens of media.

Our Vision

To become the hub of media excellence at Ontario Tech, inspiring innovation, collaboration, and creative leadership among student creators.

Our Values

Collaboration

We work together to bring ideas to life and celebrate diverse perspectives.

Creativity

We embrace imagination, boldness, and originality in everything we produce.

Community

We uplift the Ontario Tech spirit by capturing what makes our campus special.

Want to Get Involved?

Whether you’re a photographer, editor, or content creator — there’s a place for you on our team.
