Frequently Asked Questions

How much do your services cost?

Our rates vary depending on the type and duration of coverage. We offer special student and club rates. Contact us for a personalized quote.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 2-3 weeks in advance for event coverage. However, we'll do our best to accommodate last-minute requests.

What deliverables do you provide?

For photography: edited high-resolution digital images. For video: professionally edited video with music and graphics. Turnaround time is typically 5-7 business days.

Can I join your team?

Absolutely! We're always looking for talented individuals. Send us your portfolio and tell us about your skills and interests.
