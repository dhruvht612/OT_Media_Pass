What We Do

The Ontario Tech Media Pass team brings university life to the spotlight — capturing the energy, creativity, and diversity of our student community. From covering major campus events to producing digital content, we amplify student voices through media.

Videography & Editing

Media Promotion

Collaborate with event organizers and student associations to boost visibility and engagement online.
