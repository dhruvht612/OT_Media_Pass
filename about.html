About Ontario Tech Media Pass

Empowering the Ontario Tech community through professional media services

Ontario Tech Media Pass is a student-run media organization dedicated to providing high-quality photography, videography, and graphic design services to the Ontario Tech University community.

Our mission is to capture and showcase the vibrant life of our campus through professional media coverage of events, student activities, and academic achievements.

We believe in the power of visual storytelling to connect, inspire, and preserve the memories that make Ontario Tech special.
